Hi! Hola!

I am a researcher interested in how people, especially multilingual learners, interact with AI-powered learning technologies. My work bridges developmental science, language learning, cognitive neuroscience, and human-computer interaction.

I have worked on projects at Harvard University, the University of Michigan, and the Basque Center on Cognition, Brain, and Language. Across these settings, I study how children and families engage with conversational agents, interactive storybooks, speech technologies, and other learning tools.

I am especially interested in inclusive and culturally responsive design: building technologies that support multilingual learners, heritage language use, families, and educators.

Research interests

  • human-AI interaction
  • UX research for learning technologies
  • language development and multilingualism
  • child-centered design and evaluation
  • ethical AI literacy for families and educators

Education

  • Ph.D. in Cognitive Neuroscience, University of the Basque Country and BCBL
  • MSc in Cognitive Neuroscience of Language, BCBL and University of the Basque Country
  • B.A. in Cognitive Science, Indiana University Bloomington

Selected projects

  • Children's STEM learning with conversational technologies. Evaluating conversational agents that support children during interactive educational media experiences.
  • Conversational agents as bilingual reading partners. Studying English-Spanish interactive storybook experiences that support home literacy.
  • Understanding and being understood. Examining children's trust, social perception, and communication breakdowns with AI systems.
  • Generative AI and youth learning. Developing evidence to help education leaders adopt AI in responsible and inclusive ways.

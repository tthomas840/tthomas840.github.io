Hi, I am Trisha.

Trisha B. Thomas, Ph.D.

AI and UX Researcher | Learning Scientist | Cognitive Neuroscientist

I study how people, especially multilingual learners, interact with AI-powered learning technologies. My work bridges developmental science, language learning, and human-computer interaction, with a focus on inclusive, culturally responsive design.

I have led and contributed to projects at Harvard University, the University of Michigan, and the Basque Center on Cognition, Brain, and Language, collaborating with educators, designers, and researchers to create tools that support equity in learning.
